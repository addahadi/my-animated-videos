# Why every website asks you to accept cookies

- Audience: general
- Pillar: How things work ("why does this exist" series, after QR codes and CAPTCHA)
- Promise: You'll understand what a cookie is, why websites need one, and why that banner shows up everywhere.
- Target length: 90 s | Words: ~240 | Est. duration: ~92-97 s
- Assumptions: 90 s default; explains the European law as the reason for the banner (simplified, without legal detail).

## Hook options
1. كاين شركات تعرف واش قريت، واش شفت، وواش حبيت تشري... وعمرك ما دخلت للموقع نتاعهم.
2. قلبت على سبّاط في موقع... ومن بعد ولا يتبعك في كل موقع تدخلو؟ ماشي صدفة.
3. كل مرة تضغط Accept all، راك تعطي الإذن لشركات ما تعرفهاش باش يتبعوك.
(chosen: #1, a surprising and slightly unsettling fact about the viewer; beat 6 explains how it happens)

## Verify before recording
- Cookies invented in 1994 at Netscape (Lou Montulli). Well documented; a quick check is enough.
- GDPR took effect in 2018; the cookie-consent rule comes mainly from the EU ePrivacy Directive (2009 amendment). The script says "ePrivacy، ومن بعد GDPR" and marks it as simplified.
- "Reject / Necessary only" is offered by many sites, not all. The script says "في بزاف المواقع".
