## Verify before recording
- Release date and platforms: GTA 6 is set for 19 Nov 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Check on recording day that it launched as planned and that no PC or Android version has been announced since.
- Fake game downloads spreading infostealers is widely documented by security companies; the script stays general ("في بزاف الحالات") and names no specific malware.
- Stolen session cookies let an attacker use an account without the password. This is simplified: some platforms detect it or ask for re-login, so the script describes what can happen, not what always happens.
