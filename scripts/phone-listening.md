# Is your phone listening to you?

- Audience: general
- Pillar: How things work / AI explained
- Promise: Your phone almost certainly isn't recording your conversations for ads. It doesn't need to, because your location, your Wi-Fi, your activity and an AI model already predict what you want.
- Target length: 105 s | Words: 261 | Est. duration: 100 to 113 s
- Assumptions: no length was given, so I chose about 105 s (a bit over the 90 s default, because the answer has four separate causes). Shoes stay the running example the whole way through. No real company or app names appear on screen.

## Hook options
1. Story + double denial: "هدرت مع صاحبك على صبّاط... وخمس دقايق من بعد، التيليفون عمّرلك الشاشة بالصبابط. صدفة؟ لا. يسمعك؟ حتى هي لا."
2. Myth break: "قالولك التيليفون يتجسس عليك؟ الحقيقة أغرب من هكا."
3. Hidden thing: "التيليفون نتاعك ما يحتاجش يسمعك... باش يعرف واش راك حاب تشري."
(chosen: #1. Viewers recognise the moment at once. Then the two "no"s rule out both answers they already had in mind, so the only way to find out how the phone knew is to keep watching. #3 comes back as the closing line, so the video loops.)

## Delivery guide (how to say it)
Key: `/` short pause (about 0.3 s) · `//` long pause (about 0.7 s) · **bold** = stress the word · ↑ voice rises · ↓ voice drops

**Overall tone:** you're a friend at a café sharing something you found out. Sound relaxed and sure of yourself, and smile a little. Don't sound like a news reader, and don't oversell it. Energy starts high in the hook, settles into calm confidence in the middle, then slows and gets punchy at the end.

**Pronunciation:** say tech words the way Algerians say them day to day: data «داتا», server «سيرفر», model «مودال», WiFi «وايفاي», GPS «جي بي إس». Keep it natural.

**Recording tips**
- Stand up and smile while you talk. People can hear a smile in the voice.
- Hold the phone or mic about 15–20 cm from your mouth. Record in a quiet room with soft things around, such as a wardrobe full of clothes.
- Record each beat separately and leave 2 seconds of silence between them, so editing is easy.
- Do the hook 3–4 times and keep the best take. Those first 3 seconds matter most.
- Don't read flat: read the line, look away from the screen, then say it.
- Don't rush the two "لا". The silence before each one is what makes the hook work.
