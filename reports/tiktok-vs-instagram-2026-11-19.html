The short answer People watch your videos for about as long on both apps: around 38–39% are still there at 3 seconds, and they watch about 11 seconds on average. TikTok viewers even like and save at slightly higher rates. The difference is in how each app spreads a video. Instagram is currently giving Reels to lots of people who don't follow you (98% of your views), so a video with an average hook can still reach 1,000–1,700 views. TikTok first shows each video to a small test group of a few hundred people and only pushes it further if most of them watch to the end and share it. On TikTok, only 0.4–6% of viewers watch to the end of your ~90-second videos, and the videos got 5 shares in total. So every video stopped at the test group: all 11 landed between 170 and 653 views.

7 reasons TikTok views are low

Ranked by how much each one is likely holding views back. Each comes with the numbers behind it and a fix.

1 Almost nobody reaches the end of a 90-second video Biggest impact

TikTok's main signal for pushing a video beyond the first test group is how much of the video people watch and whether they rewatch it. Your videos run 64–113 seconds (92s on average), and people watch about 11 seconds, which is roughly 12% of each video. Instagram cares more about watch time in seconds, so it treats an 11-second watch as acceptable. TikTok treats a video that 98% of viewers abandon as one not worth pushing.

Watched to the end: CAPTCHA 0.4%, Claude Code router 0.4%, Selling a phone 0.6%, GTA 6 1.0%. Best: Knight Capital 5.9%. Average watch time is 4.5–19 seconds on videos lasting 64–113 seconds.
Fix: Make a separate TikTok version of each video that runs 30–50 seconds: one idea, one example, one takeaway. Split longer topics into "Part 1 / Part 2", which also brings people back to your profile. Aim for at least 15% full watches.

2 More than 60% of viewers leave in the first 3 seconds Biggest impact

Your hooks are average on both apps. Instagram still spreads average hooks right now; TikTok doesn't. The Instagram videos that broke out were the ones with the strongest openings (GTA 6: only 34% skipped; Apple fine and Phone listening: 47–48% skipped). That shows a better hook works for your channel.

TikTok, share still watching at second 1 / 3 / 10: average 68% / 38% / 18%. Worst: Untitled (Oct 21) 78% / 25% / 8%. Best: Knight Capital 74% / 49% / 24% and GTA 6 61% / 47% / 30%.
Fix: The first frame has to be the hook. Put a big on-screen question or claim in the frame from 0.0s ("تحميل GTA 6 مجانا؟ ❌"), start talking right away, and show the result or the most surprising visual first. No logo, title card or "السلام عليكم" intro. Aim for at least 55% still watching at 3s.

3 Almost no shares, so videos don't spread beyond the test group Biggest impact

On both apps, shares and DMs are the strongest sign that a video deserves more reach. Your TikTok videos got 5 shares across 11 videos, and 8 of the 11 got none. Comments are almost zero too (3 in total).

TikTok: 5 shares and 3 comments on 3,997 views. Instagram: 21 shares on 7,896 views. TikTok saves are good (47 saves, 1.2% of views), so people value the information but don't send it to anyone.
Fix: Write one line that makes people share and say it out loud near the end: "ابعثو لصاحبك اللي ديما يضغط Accept all". Ask a comment question people can answer in one word ("Accept ولا Reject؟"). Pin your own comment with the question, and reply to comments with a video reply. Each one is a new post that sends people back to the original.

4 The account is too new to get extra help Medium

TikTok has 31 followers (up from 5 a month ago) and 12 public videos. 0% of views came from the Following feed and about 93% came from For You. Every video starts from zero and depends entirely on its own test group. Instagram, meanwhile, is sending your Reels mostly to people who don't follow you.

Following feed share on all 11 videos: 0%. For You: 63–98%. Profile visits that came from videos: about 14 in the whole month.
Fix: More attempts in the first months: post 4–5 short TikTok versions a week, for example the main video plus a short clip from it. Keep one niche (everyday tech for Algerians) so TikTok learns who to show your videos to.

5 Captions and hashtags are hurting search and discovery Medium

TikTok search is a real source of views for you. "Selling a phone" got 27% of its views from Search, and GTA 6 got 7%. But two videos were posted with no caption at all, one has its hashtags stuck together, and one uses hashtags unrelated to the video.

Oct 21 and Oct 25 videos: empty caption. Knight Capital: "#R#RuntimeTechK#KnightCapitalT#TechStories…" (merged into one broken hashtag). Phone listening: #dzfashion (unrelated) and #يرئازج_كيت (Arabic letters reversed; also on Instagram).
Fix: Every caption should start with the exact words someone would search for, in Darija and French or English ("كيفاش تعرف تليفونك يسمعك – micro"). Use 3–5 hashtags about the topic, with a space between each. Say the keyword in the first sentence of the video and show it as on-screen text, because TikTok reads both.

6 The Instagram cut is posted unchanged on TikTok Medium

The same edit, length and caption style go to both apps. What made a video break out on Instagram (phone slowing down 1,452, phone listening 1,245, CAPTCHA 1,239) did nothing on TikTok (223, 193, 229). TikTok's best ones were different videos (Apple fine 653, Untitled Oct 21 617, Cookies 454). With 11 videos, that ranking is partly luck. What it does show is that copying the Instagram version isn't enough.

TikTok views range from only 170 to 653, which means none of them got past the first test group. Even TikTok's strongest openings (Knight Capital 49% at 3s) got only 414 views because full watches stayed under 6%.
Fix: Make a TikTok version of each video: shorter, faster cuts, captions placed where TikTok's buttons don't cover them, a quiet trending sound under your voice, and a cover with a short title. Make sure the export has no other app's watermark.

7 Posting times change from video to video Smaller impact

Videos went out anywhere from 09:29 to 20:45. With a small audience, the first hour decides how the test group reacts. Windsor didn't return hourly follower activity for this account yet, so this one is still an open question.

Fix: Pick one evening time (for example 19:00–21:00 Algeria time) for 3 weeks, then compare. Reply to every comment in the first 30 minutes after posting.
