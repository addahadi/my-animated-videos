A second, complementary style: "World"

An analysis of the GTA 6 police reference video and a proposal for adding it next to the current style without rebranding.

2026-11-22 · Reference: 91 s, 720 × 1280, 30 fps, about 10 scene cuts. Sampled every second; colours measured from frames.

01 · What the reference does

It turns the topic into a miniature world seen from above

Your current style shows evidence. This one shows a system at work: agents move through a space and the system reacts to them.

02 · Why it complements, not replaces

Two jobs, two styles

Current · "Report"

Light paper, cards, tags, stamps

Things sit still and get labelled. Best for evidence, comparisons, prices and decisions.

  • Why X costs more than Y
  • Which option to choose
  • What a feature really does
New · "World"

A living map, agents, overlays

Things move and the system reacts. Best for processes, tracking, flows and systems.

  • How recommendation algorithms learn what you like
  • How fraud detection or face recognition tracks you
  • How a packet travels across the internet
  • How ride or delivery apps match drivers
  • How a hacker moves through a network · game AI

The bridge already exists

Your style has a dark navy "blueprint" mode (#222B44). The reference's close-up panels are nearly the same navy (#121824 to #13274A). So "World" can be presented as the blueprint mode grown into a whole world, not a stranger.

03 · Keeping the identity

What stays, what changes, what we don't copy

Stays in both styles

  • The Runtime.tech wordmark and the same end card
  • Tajawal Arabic and the ink colour #0B0F15
  • One meaning per accent colour: red = danger, green = good, yellow = highlight, blue = info
  • Pacing: a change at least every 3 s, never a 5 s gap
  • Section 14 rules: real brand logos, no faces

Changes in "World"

  • A map canvas and navy close-up panels replace the light paper
  • A persistent overlay becomes the main storytelling device
  • Agents move: camera pans and zooms over the map are allowed, still no shake

What we won't copy

The reference's GTA wanted-level stars, police car designs and wording. We build our own vocabulary: a generic level meter, slots that fill as "what the system knows", zone overlays, and tokens drawn for each topic.

3. Name. What do we call the two styles? My pick: "Report" (current) and "World" (new).
