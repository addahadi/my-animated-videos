7 patterns behind the hits

These come up again and again across creators. Each one shows the evidence and how Runtime.tech can use it without a face on camera.

1 · Breaking news about Algeria's internet and telecoms

Internet shutdowns, outages and free-data offers. Everyone in Algeria is affected, so everyone shares them.

Internet cut (Oct 26): djalil 311K TT / 878K IG, yakoub 224K. Free month from Djezzy: 987K IG (djalil), free internet Djezzy + Ooredoo: 1.3M (yakoub).

Your angle: explain how it works. "How do you cut a whole country's internet?" "Why does 'unlimited' internet have a limit?"

2 · Prices in "ملاين"

Phone prices in Algerian money, "best phone under X ملاين", "why prices went up". This is the biggest category by far.

Realme 15T price: 10.1M. Realme Note 70: 13.2M (all-time). "حقيقة زيادة الاسعار": 375K. Best 4 phones for 4–5 ملاين: 205K.

Your angle: the explanation behind prices, not reviews. "Why the same phone costs double in Algeria" (customs, import, local assembly).

3 · Hidden phone tricks and "secret codes"

Quick wins people can try right away. Lots of saves.

"The code everyone is looking for: network error": 945K IG. Camera quality: 273K. iPhone keyboard: 200K. Block adult sites: 119K. Factory reset iPhone: 64K.

Your angle: myth-busting. "Do secret codes really fix the network?" Explain what *#06# and similar codes actually do.

4 · Algerian systems explained from the inside

BaridiMob, Algérie Poste, Sonelgaz, ATMs, OTP scams. Your exact format and close to empty. Only VisionDev does it, with a tiny account.

VisionDev: BaridiMob transfer stuck 32K (15×), Sonelgaz bill via BaridiMob 26K (12×), OTP scam 18K (8×), ATM vs app balance 10K (4.5×). His generic system design videos only get about 2.5×.

Your angle: make this a recurring series, e.g. "علاش هادي كاينة: نسخة الجزائر".

5 · Rules that change daily life

New rules about phones, internet, customs or taxes.

Phones allowed in schools: 535K, 79×, the biggest jump in the whole dataset.

Your angle: "What changes for you": the rule plus the tech behind it, posted within 24 hours.

6 · Series and reply-to-comment videos

"Part 2?" in the caption and video replies to a commenter's question bring people back and feed the algorithm.

Temu series: Part 2 got 359K with the caption "نزيدو part 2؟", Part 3 replied to a comment (102K). VisionDev answers comments with video replies.

Your angle: turn every comment question into a 30–40s TikTok cut (fits your new two-cut workflow).

7 · The cover sells the click

Every hit has a 2-line Arabic cover: a white line plus one keyword in orange or yellow, a big icon or brand mark, and a strong emotion.

djalil: "شهر انترنت مجانا / من جيزي", "قطع الانترنت على الجزائر / لا تخف". Anis Lebsir: phone + model name in yellow.

Your angle: you're faceless, so the cover text and visual have to do all the work. Use generic icons rather than real logos (as your style guide requires).
