## Risks
- Timing risk: measured clip lengths do not make word cues forced-aligned. Both internal splits and all word hits remain provisional until listening. JSON exposes that distinction.
- Meaning risk: intentional performance control is not proof of a sales motive. Separate the solid technical mechanism from the dotted purchase inference; penalties/settlements relate to disclosure, not a general proof about all phones.
- Technical-accuracy risk: battery charge, age and deliverable peak power are distinct. Water is a short analogy for delivery rate; do not imply a literal tap, a universal threshold, or a battery-health percentage that predicts exact speed.
- App/thermal risk: not every update increases load; show the optimization counterexample. RAM/working memory and file storage need different labeled trays. Heat can reduce performance even with a healthy battery.
- Layout risk: Arabic shaping/bidi and phone UI labels must be tested at portrait scale. Remotion handles all text; no Arabic text in Manim or raw SVG text. Never shrink key labels under 48 px.
- Asset risk: optional SFX/music remain unselected and unused until original or documented licensed assets exist. Use original vector phone/ad props rather than depending on licensed screenshots.
- Scope: this delivery is the script cleanup, audio inspection, storyboard and timeline. No finished video, preview or rendered cover is claimed.
