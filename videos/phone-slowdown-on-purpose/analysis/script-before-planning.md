# تليفونك ثقال… ولا ثقّلوه؟

- Audience: general
- Pillar: How things work / Software engineering
- Promise: تفهم علاش تليفونك يثقال، وواش التبطئة وحدها تثبت بلي راهم يدفعوك تشري جديد.
- Target length: 120s | Words: 292 | Est. duration: 120s
- Assumptions: Algerian Darija, Runtime.tech; two minutes for the full explanation; your cut-off app-update point is completed with growing app demands, storage and heat.

## Hook options
1. تليفونك ثقال… ولا ثقّلوه؟ (chosen: #1)
2. تفتح الكاميرا… حتى تفوت اللقطة؟
3. زعما يثقّلولك تليفونك باش تبدّلو؟

## Verify before recording
- None outstanding. Dates and technical claims were checked against the sources below.
- Beats 3–4: Apple's December 2017 acknowledgment and the later disclosure dispute are documented in the [New Jersey complaint, paragraphs 50–58](https://www.nj.gov/oag/newsreleases20/Apple-NJ-complaint.pdf). France's penalty concerned failure to inform users: [DGCCRF, 7 February 2020](https://www.economie.gouv.fr/files/files/directions_services/dgccrf/media-document/dgccrf_presse_CP-Ralentissement-fonctionnement-iPhone200207.pdf). US settlements are documented by the [California Attorney General, 18 November 2020](https://oag.ca.gov/node/196282).
- Beats 4–7 and 12: aging batteries can struggle to supply peak power; performance management can prevent shutdowns; replacement can help when battery aging is the cause. [Apple: iPhone battery and performance](https://support.apple.com/en-nz/101575).
- Beats 8–9: heavier app workloads are a possible cause, not a claim that every update slows devices or targets only new hardware. Growing task queues illustrate workload, not measured benchmarks. [Android Developers: memory and performance](https://developer.android.com/topic/performance/memory/manage-app-memory).
- Beat 10: near-full storage and temperature can affect performance. [Google: slow Android devices](https://support.google.com/android/answer/7667018?hl=en), [Apple: slow iPhone or iPad](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102598).
