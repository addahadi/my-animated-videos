Tajawal Bold is locally bundled as `media/tajawal-bold.ttf`; its license is `font-OFL.txt`. Quiet exact Runtime.tech branding. Headings 59–70px; principal labels approximately 38–53px, with 90–151px financial figures. Small context is secondary and never carries the mechanism alone.

No bottom narration captions. Short headings, version names, role labels, BUY/SELL ticket text and demonstrated completion states are functional content. Arabic is shaped by Chromium; Latin blocks use LTR. Keep stable camera, strong 4–5px near-black outlines, restrained offset shadows on meaningful objects and generous portrait space. No gradients, circuitry, stock footage, repeated decorative zooms, particle filler or full-screen cards for each sentence.

Persistent visual identities: the same numbered eight servers; written جديد/قديم badges; blue order tickets with yellow warning triangles on faulty orders; completed customer order separated from continuing outgoing tickets; broken completion feedback; the missing link between monitoring and blocking. The last gate/checklist is a conceptual lesson, not a historical installed fix.
