## Verify before recording
- None pending: date, eight servers, missed deployment, legacy code activation, rollback, trading duration and loss figures checked against the primary sources below.
- Accuracy wording: $440 million was Knight's initial announced realized pre-tax loss after closing erroneous positions; the later SEC findings put the loss above $460 million. The approximately 45 minutes describes the erroneous trading period.
- The missed server started a chain of failures. Keep the inadequate controls and harmful rollback in the story; avoid presenting it as one employee solely causing the entire loss.
- Pronunciation: Knight Capital = «نايت كابيتال»; Power Peg = «باور پِگ»; SEC = «إس إي سي». Read dollar amounts as written in Arabic, not digit by digit.
- Spoken word count includes only the Voiceover column, split on whitespace. Timing remains an estimate pending review and alignment of the supplied recordings.
