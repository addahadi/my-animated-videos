# Build notes: baridimob-scams (main cut)

- Composition: `BaridimobScams` (`src/Root.tsx`), 1080×1920, 30 fps, 2183 frames (72.77 s).
- Outputs: `out/baridimob-scams-preview.mp4` (half scale), `out/baridimob-scams.mp4` (final, H.264).
- Preview in the Studio: `npm run studio` (or `npx remotion studio src/index.tsx`), then pick Runtime-tech → BaridimobScams.
- TikTok cut: see the section at the end.

## Audio
- Voice: `public/baridimob-scams/voice.wav`, joined from `videos/barid_mob/instagram/audio1..8.ogg` with only silence trimmed (table in the plan, "Audio"). Originals untouched.
- Music: original bed synthesized locally, `videos/barid_mob/make_music.py` → `public/baridimob-scams/music.wav`, about 20 dB under the voice (music −36 dB RMS, voice −16 dB RMS), fades out over the last second.
- SFX: shared `public/sfx/` files only, cue sheet in `src/videos/baridimob-scams/sfx.ts`. No missing SFX.

## Approximate
- **Caption word timing is approximate.** Text comes from the script lines (no SRT). Words are spread over the voiced parts of each line (`videos/barid_mob/make_captions.py`); lines 2 and 8 are pinned to measured pause segments. `public/baridimob-scams/captions.srt` is also written for CapCut.
- Event times inside each line (for example the "صفر" hit at 2.3 s, the "تقبل" toggle at 41.4 s) come from pauses in the audio, not transcription. Check them by ear in the Studio.

## Deviations from the plan
- The plan's timeline props were rewritten into the project's scene contract (`canvas`, `header`, `headerTag`, `chapterPill`, `events[]`), generated by `videos/barid_mob/build_timeline.py`. Same cuts and moments.
- Scene 8: the loop shot starts at 72.1 s instead of 71.9 s, and the إيه / لا buttons appear with the question (70.75 s), so the buttons stay on screen ~1.4 s instead of ~0.6 s.
- Scene 5: the `Accessibility = إذن` label sits inside the phone's empty lower screen (light style) and leaves before the Allow dialog (40.0–41.5 s) instead of staying until 45.6 s; it overlapped the dialog otherwise. The ANDROID sticker moved to the left of the phone and hides during the 3D view.
- Scene 4: the store card says "من المتجر الرسمي" (mixed "الـ Store" rendered in the wrong order).
- Scene 7: row 3 reads "شكوى: شرطة / درك" (the longer wording wrapped to two lines).
- Scene 6 has a chapter pill "القاعدة" (not in the storyboard) for consistency with the trick scenes.

# Build notes: baridimob-scams (TikTok cut)

- Composition: `BaridimobScams-tiktok`, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 1325 frames (44.17 s). Plan: `plans/baridimob-scams-tiktok-plan.md`.
- Outputs: `out/baridimob-scams-tiktok-preview.mp4` (half scale), `out/baridimob-scams-tiktok.mp4` (final).
- Same `Video.tsx` for both cuts (`makeVideo(MAIN | TIKTOK)`). Scenes `TrickSms`, `RuleStamp` and `RecoveryChecklist` now read their own `events` prop instead of the main timeline. Main-cut stills re-rendered after the change are pixel-identical.
- Audio: `voice-tiktok.wav` from the 5 recordings, silence-trimmed only (table in the TikTok plan). Music: `make_music_tiktok.py` → `music-tiktok.wav`, ~20 dB under the voice (−36.4 vs −16.8 dB RMS).
- Captions: `captions-tiktok.json` / `public/baridimob-scams/captions-tiktok.srt`. **Word timing is approximate** (words spread inside measured phrase windows). The caption wording follows the recording, which differs a little from the script. The list is in the plan, check it by ear.
- Real logos: BaridiMob (store icon, scene 2) and Algérie Poste (Commons SVG, scene 3). Fakes stay drawn.
- Changed from the plan: none.
- Worth a human look: scene 1 (0–2.6 s) has the SMS on screen while the voice reads it, which is the longest static stretch (~3 s). Scene 5's share beat is short (2.6 s).
