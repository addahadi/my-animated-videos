# Build notes: gta6-fake-download

- **Composition:** `Gta6FakeDownload` (1080x1920, 30 fps, 1,934 frames = 64.46 s).
- **Final:** `out/gta6-fake-download.mp4`, H.264 + AAC, 64.51 s. The extra 0.05 s is AAC padding, under 2 frames.
- **Preview:** `out/gta6-fake-download-preview.mp4`, half scale.
- **Voice:** `public/gta6-fake-download/voice.wav`, a copy of `videos/GTA_6/narration-master.wav`.
  - Built from audio1-3 and audio5-9, with only edge silence trimmed.
  - audio4 is an alternate take and is not used.
  - The original recordings are untouched.
- **Music:** `public/gta6-fake-download/music.wav`, an original track synthesized locally by `videos/GTA_6/make_audio.py`.
  - A minor, 100 BPM, arranged on the timeline's sections.
  - Mixed about 20 dB under the voice (RMS), with the level baked into the file.
  - Fades out over the last second. Nothing was downloaded.
- **SFX:** all from `public/sfx/`. `buzz.wav` is new, made by the same script.
- **Captions:** `src/videos/gta6-fake-download/captions.json`, plus `public/gta6-fake-download/captions.srt` for CapCut. Generated by `videos/GTA_6/make_captions.py`.

## Approximations
- **Caption word timing is approximate.**
  - Words are spread over the voiced audio in proportion to their length.
  - Scenes 11 and 12 are pinned to measured pauses.
- **The caption text is the recorded wording**, taken from the plan's draft-ASR transcript of your Darija. It has not been checked by ear.
- **Scene event times** come from RMS pauses plus draft ASR. Check them by ear in the Studio.
- **Bidi:** "GTA 6", "GTA 6 PC" and "unknown apps" are each kept as one LTR-isolated caption token, so they read in the right order inside RTL lines.

## Deviations from the plan
- **Music** is synthesized locally instead of a "royalty-free loop". The user asked to add music, and the `remotion-builder` skill was updated to allow this.
- **Scene components:** scenes 2+3, 4+5 and 6+7 each share one component, which uses absolute timeline times so its state carries across the cut. These pairs have no exit fade and no camera push between them.
- **Scene 1:** the link card is already visible on frame 0, earlier than the 0.28 s event, so the hook reads with the sound off.
- **Scenes 5-7 were redesigned after the user's review**, because the first version was too abstract for a general audience. The plan rows were updated to match. The timeline times are unchanged, but the event `what` notes in the timeline JSON still describe the first design.
  - Scene 5: an X-ray scanner frame over the .exe, with a blurred figure inside.
  - Scene 6: an X-ray view of the file. The controller is crossed out and a thief is inside, labelled INFOSTEALER = يعني سارق. After a double-click, the thief reads YOUR browser's saved passwords: the dots turn into the real passwords and copies fly into the thief's bag, "عند السارق".
  - Scene 7: the CONNECTÉ row drops its cookie ticket, "تذكرة الدخول", into the bag.
  - The SERVER box was replaced by the bag.
- **Scene 8:** the profile card shows "نتا / @you" instead of an invented person's name.
- **End card:**
  - The follow button reads "تابع باش ما يفوتكش الجاي".
  - The tagline uses the style guide default, "TECH · EXPLAINED SIMPLY".
- **Header:** light scenes show the mono tag "TRUE OR FALSE · EP.01" plus the Arabic `SeriesPill` in the chapter-pill slot.
